## OWASP Agentic AI — Threats & Mitigations

*Version / dating:* Threat categories (T1..T15) follow the OWASP GenAI Security Project "Agentic AI — Threats and Mitigations" guide (v1.0, Feb 2025) and the related OWASP Agentic threat taxonomy circulated through 2026. OWASP has not published a fixed, officially numbered "Top 10 for Agentic AI" the way it has for LLM applications; numbering therefore varies by draft. We label every entry with a short threat NAME (numbers are indicative only) and map the threats most relevant to the control plane. Verified against the taxonomy as of 2026-07-18.

|---|---|---|---|
| T1 Memory Poisoning | ◑ partial | Context scope (partial); Retention & deletion (partial); Injection/output guardrails (partial) | Agent sees only its own scope + ancestors; siblings are not mounted, so poisoned memory cannot cross scopes (silo mode). Ingest/deliverable files age out on retentionDays; offboarding purges a scope's subtree + vault, limiting persistence of poisoned context. Heuristic inputScan flags injection shapes in ingested content before it reaches the agent. |

| T8 Repudiation & Untraceability | ✅ full | Audit ledger (full) | Append-only, hash-chained, PII-free decision ledger; any edit/delete/reorder breaks the chain (verify_audit.py). |
| T9 Identity Spoofing & Impersonation | ◑ partial | Approver RBAC + SoD (partial) | Named-approver identity-group check with segregation of duties on the /route path. |
| T10 Overwhelming Human-in-the-Loop | ◑ partial | Risk (partial); Human gates (partial) | Proportional controls gate only high-access/high-autonomy requests, so humans are not flooded with trivial approvals. Approval timeout = deny (fail-closed), so a swamped approver cannot be pressured into a rushed yes. |
| T13 Rogue Agents in Multi-Agent Systems | ◑ partial | Ethical walls (partial); Kill switch + circuit breaker (partial) | Conflicting scopes are isolated to separate runners; no shared runner across an ethical wall. ## NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)

*Version / dating:* Organized by the four AI RMF functions — Govern, Map, Measure, Manage (NIST AI 100-1, Jan 2023, plus the Generative AI Profile NIST-AI-600-1, Jul 2024). Sub-requirements are summarized in our own words, not quoted. Reviewed 2026-07-18.

| Requirement | Coverage | the control plane control(s) | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| Govern: policies, processes & accountability for AI risk | ✅ full | Risk (full); Operating mode (full) | Governance-as-code — risk/mode/RBAC policy in policy.json, enforced at one chokepoint and unit-tested (conformance_test.py). Per-scope operating mode sets the human/AI relationship deliberately per function. |
| Govern: maintain an inventory of AI systems/use cases | ✅ full | AI use-case register (full) | Derived conformity inventory of every scope's use case, owner, risk, mode, and status — cannot drift from enforcement. |
| Govern: roles, responsibilities & accountability lines | ✅ full | Approver RBAC + SoD (full) | identityGroup-based approver authorization with segregation of duties; use-case register names an accountable owner. |
| Govern: mechanisms to deactivate/decommission a system safely | ✅ full | Kill switch + circuit breaker (full) | Operator halt + automatic budget/rate breaker; offboarding removes a scope subtree and issues a deletion certificate. |
| Govern: third-party / supply-chain (provider) risk | ◑ partial | Risk (partial) | blockCrossVendor keeps confidential work on a single vendor; local-first stack avoids external data processors. |
| Map: establish context & categorize the AI use case | ✅ full | AI use-case register (full) | Each scope records its use case, owner, level, and status; lanes capture cross-scope workflows. |
| Map: identify & classify risks (likelihood/impact) | ✅ full | Risk (full) | data x access x autonomy scoring derives a risk level per request; escalates to critical when >=2 dimensions max out. |
| Map: characterize potential impacts & blast radius | ◑ partial | Context scope (partial) | Scope tree + isolation define blast radius; the access dimension encodes act-vs-read impact. |
| Measure: track effectiveness of controls & risk metrics | ◑ partial | Kill switch + circuit breaker (partial); Audit ledger (partial) | Conformance tests assert governance invariants hold; audit ledger records which controls fired per decision. Per-decision record of risk/verdict/controls is the measurement substrate (metadata only). |
| Measure: robustness & adversarial / security testing | ◑ partial | Injection/output guardrails (partial) | Heuristic input/output scans plus a security test suite and documented threat model. |
| Measure: ongoing evaluation of model quality / task-adherence over time (drift) | ✗ none | — (none) | Not built — no continuous eval or drift monitoring (roadmap E2). |
| Measure: bias / fairness measurement of outputs | ✗ none | — (none) | Not built — no bias or fairness testing harness. |
| Manage: prioritize & respond to risks (proportional treatment) | ✅ full | Risk (full); Human gates (full) | Controls switch on per-dimension (verify/approval/isolation/no-cross-vendor) proportional to the score. Human approval on ship/promote/apply; timeout = deny. |
| Manage: incident response, forensics & recovery | ◑ partial | Audit ledger (partial) | Tamper-evident decision trail supports forensic reconstruction; kill switch halts an in-progress incident. |
| Manage: formal incident-reporting workflow to stakeholders/authorities | ✗ none | — (none) | Not built — the ledger is a forensic substrate, but there is no incident-report generation or external notification. |
| Measure 2: AI performance/quality tracked and monitored over time | ◑ partial | Eval / drift lane (partial) | Agentic-leverage + verify-pass/change-failure/block metrics computed from the audit ledger, trended with drift flags (metadata proxy, not semantic eval). |
| Manage 4: monitored post-deployment; drift triggers review | ◑ partial | Eval / drift lane (partial) | Weekly eval lane emits drift WARNs to the Notify channel when key quality/risk metrics move beyond thresholds. |
| Measure 3: mechanisms to evaluate performance and catch regressions | ◑ partial | Quality regression / improvement loop (partial) | Offline eval datasets replay captured production cases and grade output (exact/contains/regex/LLM-as-judge); a regression fails the run — the quality counterpart to conformance. |
| Manage 4: incidents & feedback drive continual improvement | ◑ partial | Quality regression / improvement loop (partial) | A flagged production failure is captured as a persistent, PII-tokenized, per-scope eval case, so the fix is proven and the failure can't silently recur (governed observability). |

**Coverage summary — NIST AI RMF:** 7/19 full, 9/19 partial, 3/19 none.

- **Measure: bias / fairness measurement of outputs** — none: Not built — no bias or fairness testing harness.

- **Manage: formal incident-reporting workflow to stakeholders/authorities** — none: Not built — the ledger is a forensic substrate, but there is no incident-report generation or external notification.
